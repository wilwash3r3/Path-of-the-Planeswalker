Path of the Planeswalker — Manual

GUILDPACT INTERACTIVE PRESENTS

§1 The Story So Far

The War of the Spark is over. Ravnica is still standing — barely — and the ten guilds have gone back to what they do best: politicking, profiteering, and pretending the last invasion didn't happen. You're a nobody, a guild agent freshly assigned to enforce the Guildpact in the depths beneath the Tenth District. Then your spark ignites, and the choice of where you go stops being yours — the spark decides, one plane at a time, with no going back until the trail runs out. Someone else has lived through this before, though, and she isn't willing to let you face it alone: "We do this together, or neither of us makes it."

§3 Choosing Your Guild

Ten guilds, each with a signature ability: Azorius (Detain), Boros (Battle Cry), Dimir (Vanish), Golgari (Consume), Gruul (Savage Charge), Izzet (Spark, an area attack), Orzhov (Extort — passive, always-on gold), Rakdos (Blood Rite), Selesnya (Bloom), Simic (Adapt). Every guild also offers a Human agent or a guild-specific race with its own quirks — Vampires heal on every kill, Ghosts never need to eat or drink and can drift through locked doors alone, and so on.

§4 Survival, Combat & Rivalry

Hunger and thirst drain steadily — let either hit zero and exhaustion sets in for real. Combat is turn-based: you act, then everything nearby acts back. All ten guilds sit on a rivalry wheel, each strong against one neighbor and weak against another — know the wheel, use the wheel. Poisoned, Paralyzed, and Frightened all follow familiar tabletop-style rules; carry an Antidote.

§5 Followers & The Bag of Holding

Recruit up to two followers from contacts on the floor. Equip them, hand off a potion or ration for them to use automatically, and don't worry too much if one goes down — any rest, short or long, brings them right back and heals whatever injuries the rest of your party is carrying too. Bags of Holding are exceptionally rare and grant their own private, unlimited storage tab. You may carry more than one. You should never, under any circumstances, try to store one inside another. The label is very clear on this point.

Two copies of the same weapon, armor, or trinket can be fused into one stronger piece — vendors will always do this for a fee that climbs a little each time a piece has already been fused. The right feat lets you fuse gear yourself, straight from your pack.

§6 Field Guide

Locked doors need a key, Lockpicking, or a Ghost. Secret doors are invisible until you're close — but finding one only reveals it, glowing amber; a hidden switch somewhere nearby is what actually opens it, turning the door green once thrown. Chests are usually treasure, occasionally something wearing the shape of treasure. Water sources refill thirst for free; most are safe. Ambush rooms can seal behind you until you clear them. The boss chamber is always the biggest room on the floor, and it always seals shut the moment you walk in. Every vendor has a name too — hover them on the map to see who you're dealing with. Shop floors are safe zones — nothing can enter or approach from a distance while you're standing in one. But a foe that's already right next to you will still fight, so you're never truly stuck against one blocking a doorway.

⚠ WARNING: Contains guild rivalries, extortion, minor property damage, and at least one elder dragon planeswalker who is aware of you specifically.
