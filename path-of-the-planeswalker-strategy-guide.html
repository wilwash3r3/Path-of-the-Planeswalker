PATH OF THE PLANESWALKER

The Champion's Strategy Grimoire

Hear this, spark-touched wanderer! The multiverse does not reward the timid, and neither does this tome. Every secret bound within these pages has been tested in the fires of real combat and cross-examined against the very code that governs your fate. Read on, and walk into Amonkhet with the confidence of one who has already won.

Know Thy Foe

Mark this well, for it is the single truth that separates the reckless from the triumphant: not every horror in these halls is cut from the same cloth. Our own scrying revealed the shape of the threat you truly face — and it may astonish you.

The Feats Worth Your Oath

Choose your path wisely, for not all gifts are equal. Our counsel names these above all others:

  • Second Wind — your last defense against ruin. A burst of healing and a cleansed affliction, once between rests. Hold it in reserve for the moment true peril finds you.
  • Reserves — willpower made manifest, refilling hunger and thirst once between rests. Invaluable for those who venture far before making camp.
  • Keen Senses — widens the reach of your instinct for hidden doors. A quiet gift, but one that compounds with every floor you fully claim.
  • Artificer's Eye — transmute your surplus arms into true power without ever seeking a smith. What was clutter becomes strength.

Arming Yourself for the Long Road

Here is a truth few travelers grasp: your strength in the marketplace does not wither merely because your level has reached its peak. The finest smiths measure their wares against how much of the multiverse you have already tamed — so do not turn from the blacksmith's stall simply because your experience has run its course!

✦ Did You Know? ✦ To merge twin blades or matching plate is to fuse them into something far greater — up to four tiers of ascension await. Sell not your duplicates for scraps of gold when they might instead become legend.

Gather Your Companions

Two allies may walk beside you, each armed as fully as you yourself. Heed these truths of companionship:

  • Entrust a companion with a single draught, and they shall drink it themselves the moment their vigor falters below half. Set this once, and forget your worry.
  • Should you part ways with an ally, fear not — their arms and provisions return to your own pack, never lost.
✦ On Planeswalkers ✦ There is no separate roster of planeswalker allies — any soul listed below might turn out to be one. It changes nothing about their strength, their perk, or their words; it changes only whether they can follow you when the spark next carries you onward. An ordinary companion stays behind, bound to the plane where you found them, the moment you shift away. A planeswalker walks with you anywhere. The odds start modest — roughly one recruit in eight, early on — and climb the further you've journeyed, capped at one such discovery per plane. Don't be discouraged if your first few planes yield none; the multiverse rewards patience here more than searching.

On Rest and Ruin

A Short Rest halves what still binds your abilities. A Long Rest severs those bindings entirely and restores you whole — provided you carry food and water enough. And mark this: rest alone renews your Second Wind and your Reserves. No patience, no waiting out the hours, will return them to you otherwise.

✦ Heed This Warning ✦ Exhaustion is a thief that steals from your very vitality the longer you deny yourself rest. When the warnings come, do not press onward for "one more chamber" — that hubris has ended more journeys than any blade.

A Bag of Holding is rarer than any relic in these halls — expect no more than two across an entire pilgrimage. When fortune grants you one, fill it with your bulkiest draughts and provisions, not idle trinkets.

A Hidden Door Demands a Search

Mark this well, for it has caught many a hasty adventurer off guard: finding a hidden door is only half the trial. The door glows amber once your instincts (or your Keen Senses) reveal it — but it remains sealed shut. Somewhere in that very room or the corridor leading to it waits a hidden switch, invisible until the door itself is found. Only by discovering and triggering that switch will the door turn green and yield its passage.

✦ A Word of Counsel ✦ Do not simply walk away from a sealed hidden door in frustration — the switch that opens it is always somewhere close at hand, guaranteed to be reachable. Search the room thoroughly before moving on.

Keen Senses now reaches a full four tiles instead of three, granting more warning before you stumble upon a hidden door — precious time better spent scouring the room for its switch.

Sanctuary Has Its Limits

A vendor's floor is sanctuary from a distance — no horror will cross into it, and none will approach you from afar while you linger there. Guild abilities and spells still cannot be loosed from within its bounds; those reach too far to count as fair combat from safety. But this protection has an edge: a creature already standing at your side, blade to blade, will still swing at you — and you may swing back plainly, with your own weapon, as in any other fight.

✦ A Word of Counsel ✦ Should some creature plant itself in the very corridor leading from your sanctuary — often after a hidden door lays bare a path you didn't expect — do not despair. Simply strike it down as you would any foe. Sanctuary keeps distant threats at bay; it was never meant to leave you defenseless against one already within arm's reach.

The Sealed Chamber Shows No Mercy

Take this to heart above all: an ambush chamber or a boss's sanctum seals every door the instant your presence is known — not merely one. There is no slipping free to gather your strength once you have crossed the threshold. Spend your cooldowns and your potions before you enter, for once sealed, only victory or ruin awaits.

The Law of the Multiverse

Before the great work is finished, the spark's path follows strict and ancient rule — know it now, lest you mistake fate for malfunction:

  • Five trials must be completed upon your current plane before the spark will carry you onward at all.
  • Your destination is never yours to choose — the spark alone decides where you land.
  • Once you have left a plane behind, you may not return to it by will alone — not until the final reckoning is complete. This is no flaw in the weave. It is the tale itself.

Yet when Bolas at last falls, the multiverse opens wide before you — any plane, any moment, Ravnica included, entirely by your own choosing.

The Final Reckoning

Amonkhet's gates shatter once you have proven your worth across the six planes beyond it — and from that moment, every shift carries you straight toward your destiny, chance no longer a factor.

Here is the truth few expect: Bolas measures his own might against your level alone, not the depths you have plundered. No amount of extra floor-crawling will soften this blow — so arm yourself well rather than grind in vain. Enter with your Second Wind held ready, your finest gear upon you, and your guild's own signature power primed to strike.

Beyond the Reckoning: The Endless Road

Should you triumph, the road need not end! But know this — your level cannot climb past its fiftieth rung, and once all six planes lie conquered, that well of growth runs dry too. The horrors below, however, know no such limit, and grow mightier with every step you take. In time you shall find the depth beyond which even you cannot pass. This is no failing of the design — it is the shape of the challenge itself. Claim your depth, and wear it with pride.

✦ He Has Not Forgotten ✦ Do not grow too comfortable in your victory. Nicol Bolas is patient, and patience does not forgive. From time to time, on any plane but the one where you first struck him down, he will find you again — tougher than before, and never announcing himself gently. Defeat him again, and he'll yield a fine reward for the trouble. He always comes back eventually.
