  • FIXWaterskin never got a purchase quantity the way Rations did — always sold one at a time.
  • FIXGuild spell cooldown never reset on Short or Long Rest, only the base guild ability did.

  • FIXUrza's free travel could take you back to an already-cleared plane, but the 5-missions-to-leave gate only ever exempted the endgame "multiverse saved" state — never Urza's cheat — so leaving again required re-grinding missions on a plane you'd already finished.

  • FIXGear scaling in shops (blacksmith weapons/armor, trinkets) leaned almost entirely on player level, which hard-caps at 10 — meaning gear stopped improving for the back half of the game. Added a planes-cleared scaling term to both gear and enemies so the curve keeps climbing toward the final fight instead of flatlining.
  • FIXTrainer NPCs re-rolled their second skill option every single visit, letting the player leave and return repeatedly until a desired combo appeared. The offer is now cached on the NPC the first time it's generated and stays fixed on every later visit.
  • FIXDisabled Buy/Merge buttons gave zero feedback when a purchase was simply unaffordable, reading identically to the "everything is broken" dialogue bug from earlier. Since native disabled buttons don't fire clicks at all, switched insufficient-funds cases to a dimmed-but-clickable state and added a clear "not enough gold" message on click.
  • FIXAmbush and boss rooms only ever sealed one exit even when a room had multiple corridor connections, leaving a second way to slip out mid-fight. Added a proper room-perimeter scan that finds and seals every connection, not just one.
  • FIXCaught while fixing the above: the code that releases a seal once a fight is won used .find() — only the first sealed door. Fixing the seal-everything bug without this would have left every door but one permanently locked forever. Fixed both release points to unseal every door that was sealed.
  • FIXEnemy icons had zero contrast backing and could visually vanish into a plane's background palette (a dark vampire against Innistrad's dark tiles, for one). Added a two-tone halo — dark disc plus a light ring — behind every enemy so it stays visible regardless of what's underneath.
  • FIXThe first pass at a map zoom used a plain CSS scale on a fixed center point, so zooming in while off-center could push the player character out of view entirely. Replaced with a proper canvas camera transform that always keeps the player dead-center on screen. Also had to fix the secret-door sparkle effect and mouse-hover tile detection, both of which would have misaligned once a real camera transform was introduced.

Near a map edge while zoomed in, the transformed clear rectangle no longer covered the full canvas, leaving stale pixels from the previous frame on screen — visible as a ghost "second player" marker.   • FIXDowned followers had no marker on the full floor Map screen at all — healthy or downed, followers were never drawn there, even though the hover tooltip already correctly reported their status.   • FIXA safe zone bordering a dangerous room (often revealed by a secret door) let the player attack enemies for free — enemies can never step onto a safe tile and freeze entirely while the player stands on one, but nothing stopped the player's own melee attack, guild ability, or guild spell from reaching out and hitting them anyway. Closed all three: dealing damage from a safe zone is now blocked, matching the "no enemies will follow you in" sanctuary design.

A distant enemy still can't camp a safe zone's doorway, but one already standing next to you will fight back — which restores mutual risk without needing any special-case workaround, and removed the now-unnecessary attack block and shove-past logic entirely.
